# Field validation worksheet

**Status: fieldwork pending.** The synthetic route distances and case outcomes can be reproduced in the demo.

Choose a corridor with a clinic, transit stop, and alternate path. Aim for 15–25 segments. Use [MoHUA’s accessibility guidance](https://depwd.gov.in/en/document/harmonised-guidelines-standards-for-universal-accessibility-in-india-2021/) to inform observations. Record what you directly observe and mark uncertainties. Keep consent records and raw notes private. Get permission to film private spaces and blur identifiable faces and plates in public footage unless you have the appropriate consent.

Interview 6–8 people with varied mobility needs. Ask about the last time a barrier changed a necessary trip, how they found an alternative, and what evidence would convince them to use the original route again. Use anonymous participant codes. Record actual sample size, recurring themes, and product decisions.

Run the reporting task and the route-interpretation task with five participants. Aim for four of five to finish both unassisted, each under 90 seconds. Report the actual counts and one design update from feedback.
